SUBJECT: Service Campaign for Maruti Suzuki Swift ZXi and Baleno Alpha Models
— Airbag Control Unit Software Update

Dear Valued Customer,

Maruti Suzuki India Limited is proactively reaching out to owners of certain Swift ZXi and Baleno Alpha models for a complimentary software update of the Airbag Control Unit (ACU).

AFFECTED MODELS:
- Maruti Suzuki Swift ZXi (Petrol, 1.2L DualJet) — MY 2023-2025
- Maruti Suzuki Baleno Alpha (Petrol, 1.2L DualJet) — MY 2023-2025

ISSUE DESCRIPTION:
A potential software calibration issue in the Airbag Control Unit may cause delayed airbag deployment response in certain rare collision scenarios. This is a precautionary update — no accidents or injuries have been reported.

NUMBER OF VEHICLES AFFECTED:
Approximately 32,000 vehicles across India

HOW TO CHECK IF YOUR VEHICLE IS AFFECTED:
- Visit www.marutisuzuki.com/recall-check
- Enter your Vehicle Registration Number (e.g., MH20DV2366, DL07AB1234)
- Or call Maruti Care: 1800-102-1800 (Toll Free)
