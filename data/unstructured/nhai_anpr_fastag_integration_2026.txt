NATIONAL HIGHWAYS AUTHORITY OF INDIA (NHAI)
ELECTRONIC TOLL COLLECTION DIVISION

CIRCULAR: NHAI/ETC/2026/ANPR-INTEGRATION/892
Date: 8 November 2026

SUBJECT: Integration of ANPR Vehicle Identification with FASTag Electronic Toll Collection System for Enhanced Vehicle Verification

1. OBJECTIVE
This circular outlines the technical framework for integrating Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) systems with FASTag toll collection infrastructure at all National Highway toll plazas operated by NHAI.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
(a) All toll plazas shall install high-resolution ANPR cameras (minimum 4K resolution) capable of reading Indian standard number plates at vehicle speeds up to 120 km/h.
(b) ANPR readings shall be cross-verified against FASTag vehicle registration data in real-time.
(c) Mismatches between ANPR-detected plate and FASTag-registered plate shall trigger an automatic alert.

3. DATABASES TO BE INTEGRATED
The ANPR-FASTag integration system shall query the following databases:
(a) Vahan (National Vehicle Registration Database) — for vehicle registration verification
(b) Sarathi (National Driving License Database) — for driver verification
(c) NCRB Stolen Vehicle Database — for stolen vehicle detection
(d) IRDAI Motor Insurance Database — for insurance validity check
(e) MoRTH Fitness Certificate Database — for vehicle fitness status

4. TOLL PLAZAS COVERED (Phase 1)
- Delhi-Jaipur Highway (NH-48): DND Flyway, Manesar Toll, Shahjahanpur Toll
- Delhi-Mumbai Expressway: Sohna Toll, Dausa Toll, Ratlam Toll
- Mumbai-Pune Expressway: Khalapur Toll, Urse Toll, Talegaon Toll
- Bangalore-Chennai Expressway: Electronic City Toll, Vellore Toll
- Delhi-Agra Expressway (Yamuna Expressway): Noida Toll, Mathura Toll

5. DATA SHARING PROTOCOL
(a) ANPR data shall be shared with State Police in real-time for stolen vehicle detection
(b) Insurance status data shall be shared with IRDAI for compliance monitoring
(c) All data sharing shall comply with Information Technology Act 2000 and Motor Vehicles Act 1988
(d) Vehicle capture data retention period: 90 days (extendable to 180 days for flagged vehicles)

6. PERFORMANCE METRICS
Target accuracy for ANPR plate recognition: 95% or higher
Maximum processing time per vehicle: 2 seconds
Integration latency with external databases: Under 100 milliseconds

Issued by: Member (Technical), NHAI
