ROAD ACCIDENT ANALYSIS REPORT — DELHI NCR
Prepared by: Indian Road Safety Campaign (IRSC)
Period: October — November 2026

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report analyzes road accident patterns in the Delhi NCR region for the October-November 2026 period, with specific focus on uninsured vehicles, overspeed incidents, and vehicle fitness-related accidents.

1. ACCIDENT STATISTICS (Oct-Nov 2026)
Total accidents reported: 2,847
Fatal accidents: 312 (11%)
Accidents involving uninsured vehicles: 423 (15%)
Accidents involving vehicles without valid fitness certificate: 187 (7%)
Overspeed-related accidents: 891 (31%)

2. UNINSURED VEHICLE INVOLVEMENT
Of the 423 accidents involving uninsured vehicles:
- 67% were two-wheelers (MCWG category)
- 18% were SUV/Private category vehicles
- 10% were commercial vehicles (HGV/MGV)
- 5% were hatchbacks/sedans

Key finding: SUV category vehicles without insurance had 2.3x higher accident severity compared to insured SUVs of the same class.

3. VEHICLE FITNESS CORRELATION
Vehicles with expired fitness certificates showed:
- 40% higher brake failure rate
- 25% higher tire blowout rate
- 60% higher emission-related mechanical failure

Manufacturers with highest fitness failure rates:
- Older Mahindra Scorpio models (pre-2020): 23% fitness failure
- Tata Motors commercial vehicles: 19% fitness failure
- Maruti Suzuki Alto/WagonR (pre-2018): 15% fitness failure
- Hyundai models: 8% fitness failure (lowest among mass-market)

4. OVERSPEED HOTSPOTS
Top locations for overspeed violations (detected via ANPR):
- DND Flyway (average violation speed: 102 km/h in 80 km/h zone)
- Dwarka Expressway (average: 97 km/h in 70 km/h zone)
- Mumbai-Pune Expressway (average: 118 km/h in 100 km/h zone)
- Outer Ring Road, Bangalore (average: 89 km/h in 60 km/h zone)

5. VEHICLE COLOR AND VISIBILITY
Analysis of accident frequency by vehicle color:
- BLACK vehicles: 22% of accidents (over-represented vs 15% fleet share)
- WHITE vehicles: 28% of accidents (proportional to 30% fleet share)
- SILVER/GREY vehicles: 20% of accidents
- RED vehicles: 8% of accidents (lower visibility factor debunked)

6. RECOMMENDATIONS
(a) ANPR networks should automatically flag uninsured vehicles and generate Ministry reports
(b) Vehicles with expired fitness certificates should be prevented from renewing insurance
(c) Overspeed detection should trigger automatic alerts to vehicle owners and enforcement agencies
(d) Insurance companies should offer premium discounts for vehicles with active ANPR-verified compliance records

Data Sources: Delhi Traffic Police, MoRTH Vahan Database, IRDAI Motor Insurance Database
