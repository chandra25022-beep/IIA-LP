INSURANCE REGULATORY AND DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY OF INDIA (IRDAI)
CIRCULAR No: IRDAI/NL/CIR/MOT/2026/198

Date: 1 November 2026
To: All General Insurance Companies

SUBJECT: Mandatory Comprehensive Motor Insurance Coverage for SUV Category Vehicles in Delhi NCR — Compliance Directive

1. BACKGROUND
In light of the increasing number of uninsured SUV category vehicles involved in road accidents in the Delhi NCR region, IRDAI hereby issues the following compliance directive effective from 1 January 2027.

2. DIRECTIVE
(a) All SUV, MUV, and Premium SUV category vehicles registered in Delhi (DL), Gurgaon (HR-26), Faridabad (HR-29), Noida (UP-16), and Ghaziabad (UP-14) RTO zones MUST maintain active Comprehensive Motor Insurance coverage (not merely Third Party liability).

(b) Insurance companies shall flag policies with "Third Party Only" coverage for SUV category vehicles in the above jurisdictions and issue renewal notices recommending upgrade to Comprehensive coverage.

(c) Vehicles found operating without valid Comprehensive coverage in Delhi NCR will be subject to enhanced penalties under the Motor Vehicles (Amendment) Act 2019:
   - First offense: Fine of INR 5,000
   - Repeat offense: Fine of INR 10,000 + vehicle seizure for 7 days

3. CLAIM SETTLEMENT
(a) All motor claims involving SUV vehicles with active Comprehensive policies must be settled within 30 days of surveyor report submission.
(b) Zero Depreciation Bumper-to-Bumper claims for vehicles less than 3 years old shall not be rejected on grounds of depreciation.

4. PREMIUM GUIDELINES
Recommended premium bands for SUV Comprehensive coverage:
- Engine capacity up to 1500cc: INR 12,000 — 18,000 per annum
- Engine capacity 1500cc — 2000cc: INR 18,000 — 28,000 per annum
- Engine capacity above 2000cc: INR 28,000 — 48,000 per annum

5. REPORTING
All insurers must submit monthly reports to IRDAI on:
- Number of SUV policies issued/renewed in Delhi NCR
- Percentage of Comprehensive vs Third Party coverage
- Claim settlement ratios

Insurers referenced: HDFC ERGO General Insurance, ICICI Lombard General Insurance, Bajaj Allianz General Insurance, Tata AIG General Insurance, New India Assurance Co., National Insurance Co., SBI General Insurance, Reliance General Insurance, United India Insurance Co.

This circular is effective immediately.
