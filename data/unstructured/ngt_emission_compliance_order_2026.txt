NATIONAL GREEN TRIBUNAL (NGT)
PRINCIPAL BENCH, NEW DELHI

ORDER No: NGT/OA/2026/1847/ENV

In the matter of:
Air Quality Monitoring and Vehicle Emission Compliance in Delhi NCR

SUBJECT: Directions for Enforcement of BS-VI Emission Standards and
Identification of High-Polluting Vehicles through ANPR Integration

Date: 10 November 2026

BACKGROUND:
The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) has reported that vehicular emissions account for approximately 40% of PM2.5 pollution in Delhi NCR during the winter months (October-February). Despite the introduction of BS-VI emission norms, a significant number of vehicles operating in Delhi NCR do not comply with current emission standards.

DIRECTIONS:

1. ANPR-EMISSION INTEGRATION
   The Delhi Transport Department shall integrate its ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition) camera network with the Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate database to enable real-time identification of vehicles without valid PUC certificates.

2. VEHICLE CATEGORIES OF CONCERN
   The following vehicle categories shall receive enhanced scrutiny:
   - Diesel vehicles older than 10 years in Delhi NCR
   - BS-IV or lower emission norm vehicles of any fuel type
   - Commercial vehicles (HGV, MGV, Bus) without valid PUC certificates
   - SUV and MUV category vehicles with engine capacity above 2000cc (diesel)

3. PENALTIES
   - Operating without valid PUC: INR 10,000 fine
   - BS-IV non-compliant vehicles in NCR: INR 5,000 fine per offense
   - Repeat offenders: Vehicle impounding for 7 days

4. FUEL TYPE ANALYSIS
   The following fuel types shall be monitored:
   - DIESEL: Highest emission concern, mandatory PUC every 3 months for vehicles > 5 years
   - PETROL: Standard PUC requirement every 6 months
   - CNG: Lower emission, PUC every 12 months
   - ELECTRIC: Exempt from PUC requirements
   - HYBRID: Same as PETROL category

5. AFFECTED MANUFACTURERS
   Vehicles from the following manufacturers have been identified with highest non-compliance rates:
   - Mahindra (diesel SUVs — Scorpio, Bolero, XUV series)
   - Tata Motors (commercial vehicles — Ace, 407 series)
   - Toyota (diesel SUVs — Fortuner, Innova older models)
   - Ashok Leyland (buses and heavy commercial vehicles)

6. GEOGRAPHICAL ZONES
   Enhanced enforcement zones: DL-01 to DL-10 (Delhi), UP-14 (Ghaziabad), UP-16 (Noida), HR-26 (Gurugram), HR-29 (Faridabad)

The Delhi Government shall submit a compliance report within 30 days.

Chairperson, National Green Tribunal
