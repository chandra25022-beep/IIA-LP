SUBJECT: Mandatory 10,000 km Service Interval Reminder for Tata Nexon and Harrier Models

Dear Tata Motors Vehicle Owner,

This bulletin serves as a reminder for all Tata Nexon Fearless and Tata Harrier XZA+ owners to adhere to the recommended service intervals for optimal vehicle performance and safety.

APPLICABLE MODELS:
- Tata Nexon Fearless (1.2L Turbo Petrol / 1.5L Diesel) — MY 2022-2026
- Tata Harrier XZA+ (2.0L Diesel) — MY 2022-2026
- Tata Safari Accomplished (2.0L Diesel) — MY 2022-2026

SERVICE SCHEDULE:
- First service: 1,000 km or 1 month (whichever is earlier) — FREE
- Second service: 10,000 km or 6 months — Paid
- Third service: 20,000 km or 12 months — Paid
- Subsequent: Every 10,000 km or 6 months

CRITICAL MAINTENANCE ITEMS:
1. Engine oil and filter replacement
2. Brake pad inspection (front and rear)
3. Tire rotation and pressure check
4. Suspension linkage inspection
5. Battery health check
6. Software update for infotainment and ADAS systems

Delhi NCR: Tata Motors, Dwarka (DL-07) | Tata Motors, Noida (UP-16) | Tata Motors, Gurgaon (HR-26)
Mumbai: Tata Motors, Thane (MH-04) | Tata Motors, Pune (MH-12)
Bangalore: Tata Motors, Whitefield (KA-03) | Tata Motors, Mysore (KA-09)
Chennai: Tata Motors, OMR (TN-09) | Tata Motors, Anna Nagar (TN-01)

IMPORTANT WARNING:
Vehicles that have not completed the recommended 10,000 km service may experience:
- Reduced fuel efficiency
- Premature brake wear
- Potential suspension issues
- Voided warranty for related components
