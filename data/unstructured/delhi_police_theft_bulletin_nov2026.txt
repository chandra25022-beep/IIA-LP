SUBJECT: Surge in Vehicle Theft — Hyundai Creta and Maruti Swift Models in South-West Delhi

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Delhi Police Traffic Division has observed a 35% increase in vehicle thefts during October-November 2026, primarily targeting Hyundai Creta SUV and Maruti Suzuki Swift hatchback models in the South-West Delhi region (Dwarka, Sarita Vihar, Vasant Kunj).

2. MODUS OPERANDI
Organized gangs operating in the DL-07 (Dwarka) and DL-09 (Janakpuri) RTO zones are using sophisticated relay attacks on keyless entry systems. Vehicles are typically stolen between 11 PM and 4 AM from residential colonies and apartment parking areas.

3. AFFECTED AREAS
- Sector 7, Dwarka (highest concentration — 8 cases in October)
- Pocket A, Sarita Vihar (5 cases)
- Vasant Kunj Sector B (4 cases)
- Janakpuri C-Block (3 cases)

4. VEHICLE PROFILES
Most targeted vehicles:
- Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT (BLACK and WHITE variants) — 12 cases
- Maruti Suzuki Swift ZXi (WHITE) — 8 cases
- Mahindra Scorpio-N Z8L — 4 cases
- Toyota Fortuner 4x4 — 3 cases

5. RECOVERY STATUS
Of 27 vehicles stolen in this period:
- 8 recovered (30%) from Faridabad-Palwal Highway corridor
- 4 recovered from Gurgaon Industrial Area (stripped for parts)
- 15 still missing — alerts broadcast on ANPR network

6. RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
- All ANPR checkpoints in DL-07, DL-09, DL-10 zones to maintain HIGH alert
- Cross-reference captured vehicles against stolen database in real-time
- Vehicles matching Hyundai Creta BLACK/WHITE profile to be flagged for verification
- Coordinate with Haryana and UP Police for inter-state recovery operations

7. INTER-STATE COORDINATION
FIR cross-references shared with:
- Haryana Police (HR-26 Gurugram, HR-29 Faridabad jurisdictions)
- UP Police (UP-14 Ghaziabad, UP-16 Noida jurisdictions)
- Rajasthan Police (RJ-14 Jaipur — suspected vehicle laundering network)
