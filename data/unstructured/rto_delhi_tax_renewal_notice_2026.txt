REGIONAL TRANSPORT OFFICE — DELHI
NOTIFICATION TO VEHICLE OWNERS

Ref: RTO/DL/2026/TAX-RENEWAL/4521
Date: November 2026

SUBJECT: Annual Road Tax Renewal and Registration Status Update for Delhi-Registered Vehicles

NOTICE:

The Regional Transport Offices of Delhi (DL-01 through DL-10) hereby notify all vehicle owners that the annual road tax renewal cycle for the financial year 2026-27 is now open.

1. TAX RENEWAL DEADLINES
   - Private vehicles (LMV, Sedan, Hatchback, SUV): 31 March 2027
   - Commercial vehicles: 31 December 2026
   - Two-wheelers: 30 June 2027

2. REGISTRATION STATUS
   Vehicles with unpaid road tax for more than 6 months will have their registration status changed from "ACTIVE" to "SUSPENDED". Suspended vehicles detected by ANPR cameras will be automatically flagged for enforcement action.

3. ONLINE PAYMENT
   Road tax can be paid online through:
   - Parivahan portal: https://parivahan.gov.in
   - mParivahan mobile app
   - RTO office counters (DL-01 Mall Road, DL-07 Dwarka, DL-09 Janakpuri, DL-10 Raja Garden)

4. SPECIAL PROVISIONS
   (a) Electric vehicles (EV) registered in Delhi are EXEMPT from road tax until 2030.
   (b) Hybrid vehicles receive a 50% road tax concession.
   (c) CNG-fitted vehicles receive a 25% concession.

5. HYPOTHECATION CLEARANCE
   Vehicle owners who have completed loan repayment must obtain hypothecation removal endorsement (RC update) from the financing bank and submit form RTO-HPT at the respective RTO office.

6. INTER-STATE TRANSFER
   Vehicles being transferred from Delhi to another state require:
   - NOC (No Objection Certificate) from Delhi RTO
   - Valid insurance and fitness certificate
   - Clearance of all pending challans and fines

RTO Offices: DL-01 North Delhi (Mall Road), DL-02 New Delhi (Tilak Marg), DL-07 South West Delhi (Dwarka), DL-09 West Delhi (Janakpuri), DL-10 West Delhi (Raja Garden)
