HYUNDAI MOTOR INDIA LIMITED
VOLUNTARY SAFETY RECALL CAMPAIGN

Recall Notice No: HMIL/RECALL/2026/CR-1847
Date of Issue: 15 October 2026

SUBJECT: Precautionary Recall — Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT (Manufacture Year 2022-2025)
Brake Caliper Inspection and Front Suspension Lower Arm Replacement

Dear Vehicle Owner,

Hyundai Motor India Limited is conducting a voluntary safety recall campaign for certain Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT models manufactured between January 2022 and March 2025. This recall affects approximately 48,000 vehicles across India.

AFFECTED MODELS:
- Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT SX (Petrol)
- Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT SX(O) (Petrol)
- Vehicle Class: SUV / Private

ISSUE DESCRIPTION:
During quality inspections, Hyundai identified a potential issue with the front brake caliper mounting bolts and front suspension lower arm ball joints in the affected models. In certain driving conditions (high speed, rough terrain), the brake caliper mounting bolts may experience premature wear, potentially leading to reduced braking efficiency.

RECOMMENDED ACTION:
All owners of affected Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT vehicles are requested to visit their nearest authorized Hyundai service center for a FREE inspection and replacement of the affected components.

IDENTIFICATION:
Your vehicle is affected if:
- Model: Creta 1.6 VTVT
- Manufacturing year: 2022 to 2025
- Engine Number prefix: ENG-DL, ENG-MH, ENG-KA, ENG-UP, ENG-TN

SERVICE CENTERS:
- Delhi NCR: Hyundai Dwarka (DL-07), Hyundai Noida (UP-16), Hyundai Gurgaon (HR-26)
- Mumbai: Hyundai Andheri (MH-02), Hyundai Thane (MH-04)
- Bangalore: Hyundai Koramangala (KA-01), Hyundai Whitefield (KA-03)
- Chennai: Hyundai T. Nagar (TN-07), Hyundai OMR (TN-09)

IMPORTANT: This is a precautionary recall. If your vehicle's registration number starts with DL, MH, KA, UP, or TN state codes and the model is Creta, please schedule an appointment immediately.
